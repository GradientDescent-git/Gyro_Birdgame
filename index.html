Gesture Controls Guide

  • Aim: Move your index finger in front of the camera.
  • Grab: Pinch your thumb and index finger together near the slingshot.
  • Pull & Slingshot: Move your pinched hand backward relative to the grab anchor.
  • Launch: Release the pinch gesture.
  • Fallback: Click and drag with mouse if webcam is unavailable.
